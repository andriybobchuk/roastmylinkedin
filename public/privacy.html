Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • The LinkedIn username or URL you submit
  • The public profile data returned about that URL by our data provider
  • Your email address, if you choose to give us one for delivery of the paid report
  • Basic technical logs (IP, user agent, timestamps) for security and abuse prevention
  • Payment details are handled entirely by Stripe. We never see your card.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your LinkedIn login credentials. We do not log into LinkedIn on your behalf. We do not use tracking pixels, third-party advertising cookies, or session replay tools.

Third-party subprocessors

  • Apify — retrieves the public LinkedIn profile data. See Apify's privacy policy.
  • Google (Gemini) / Anthropic (Claude) — generates the audit and rewrites. Profile data is sent to these providers for analysis. See Google's and Anthropic's policies.
  • Stripe — payment processing. See Stripe's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Public profile data is stored in memory during your audit and discarded within 24 hours unless you purchased a report, in which case we retain the audit for 90 days so you can re-access it. Email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.

Cookies

Our current build sets no cookies at all. When we add analytics, we will use only strictly-necessary and analytics cookies. You will be prompted to accept or reject non-essential cookies before any are set. Your choice is stored in localStorage and you can change it at any time via the "Cookies" link in our footer.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU or UK, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent for processing, to receive your data in a portable format, and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (in Poland: UODO). Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

If you are the subject of a profile someone else analyzed and you want the record deleted, email us with the LinkedIn URL and we'll purge it within 30 days.

Your rights (CCPA — California residents)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Do we sell or share your data? No. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To formally register a "Do Not Sell / Share My Data" preference, email us at hello@roastmylinkedin.net with the subject line "CCPA opt-out".
