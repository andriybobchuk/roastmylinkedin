Privacy Policy

Data controller

The data controller is Andrii Bobchuk, based in Warsaw, Poland. Contact: andriybobchuk@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • The LinkedIn username or URL you submit
  • Public profile data returned about that URL by our data provider (headline, About, experience, skills, activity, publications, patents, and similar public fields)
  • Your email address, if you provide one for delivery of a paid report
  • Basic technical logs (IP, user agent, timestamps) for security and abuse prevention — retained 30 days
  • Payment details are handled entirely by Stripe. We never see your card.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your LinkedIn login credentials. We do not log into LinkedIn on your behalf. We do not use tracking pixels, third-party advertising cookies, or session-replay tools.

Third-party subprocessors

  • Apify — retrieves the public LinkedIn profile data. See Apify's privacy policy.
  • Google (Gemini) and/or Anthropic (Claude) — generate the audit and rewrites. Profile data is sent to whichever provider is configured, for the analysis request only. See Google's and Anthropic's policies.
  • Netlify — hosts the site and the job store used to hand off analysis state. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Stripe — payment processing. See Stripe's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Public profile data is stored in the job queue (Netlify Blobs) during your audit and discarded within 24 hours unless you purchase a report, in which case we retain the audit for 90 days so you can re-access it. Email addresses (paid customers) are retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.

Cookies

Our current build sets no cookies at all. If we add analytics later, we will use only strictly-necessary and analytics cookies and prompt you to accept or reject non-essential ones.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the EU, UK, or another jurisdiction that recognizes similar rights, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data; to withdraw consent for processing; to receive your data in a portable format; and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (in Poland: UODO). Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

If you are the subject of a profile that someone else analyzed and you want the record deleted, email us with the LinkedIn URL and we will purge it within 30 days.
